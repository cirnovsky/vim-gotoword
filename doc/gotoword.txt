*gotoword.txt*  Goto a word in your sight

Author:  Guanyu Wang <https://guanyu.me/>
License: Same terms as Vim itself (see |license|)

Jump to a word sitting on the screen.  Label every word with a two-letter
tag.

						*:Gotoword*
:Gotoword		Label every word with >= 2 length with a two-letter
			tag.

HIGHLIGHTING					*gotoword-highlighting*

Labels are shown with highlight group |gotowordHighlight|.

Defaults adapt to |'background'|. You can override colors/styles:
>
	let g:gotoword_guifg = '#cdd6f4'
	let g:gotoword_guibg = '#313244'
	let g:gotoword_gui = 'underline'
	let g:gotoword_ctermfg = 'White'
	let g:gotoword_ctermbg = 'DarkBlue'
	let g:gotoword_cterm = 'underline'
<

Notes:
- `gui*` values support hex colors.
- `cterm*` values must be terminal color names or numbers.
